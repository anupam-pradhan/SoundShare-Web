SoundShare: Audio Sharing

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 • Package: com.soundshare.soundshare
🛡️
In short: your audio is never recorded, stored, or uploaded — it only travels over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot to the devices you invite. SoundShare is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, and uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. We never sell your data.

1 Overview

SoundShare ("we", "our", or "the App"), published by Quick Media Solution, lets you share your phone's audio with Bluetooth headphones and with friends' devices on the same Wi-Fi network. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed, by whom, and the choices you have.

2 Information We Collect and How It Is Used

  • No account or personal details: SoundShare has no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Your audio stays private: When you use Wi-Fi sharing, the sound playing on your phone is captured in memory and sent directly over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot to the browsers of people you invite. It is never recorded, saved, or sent to us or any server.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions. Ads may be personalised where you have agreed to it.
  • App analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): We receive anonymous usage statistics such as which features are used, session length, app version, device model, and crashes, linked to a random app-instance ID — not to your identity.

3 Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

SoundShare requests only the Android permissions needed for audio sharing, ads and analytics:

📡 Nearby Devices & Bluetooth (`BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`)

Required to discover, pair with, and stream synchronized audio to multiple Bluetooth headphones and speakers simultaneously.

🎙️ Audio Capture (`RECORD_AUDIO`) & Screen Audio Sharing

Android requires this permission, plus your confirmation in its "start sharing" dialog, before an app can capture the sound other apps are playing. SoundShare uses it only while you are sharing over Wi-Fi, to stream that sound to the devices you invite. The microphone is never used and nothing is stored.

⚡ Foreground Service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION`)

Keeps sharing running while your screen is locked or you use other apps, and shows an ongoing notification so you always know sharing is active.

🌐 Internet & Network State (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)

Used to stream audio to devices on your local network, to load ads, and to send anonymous analytics.

📳 Vibration (`VIBRATE`)

Gives a light tap of feedback when you press buttons in the app.

🔔 Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)

Displays active playback session controls (Play, Pause, Disconnect) in your system notification shade.

4 Third-Party Services & Your Choices

SoundShare uses these Google services, each governed by Google's own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — ads. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — usage statistics. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play services — in-app reviews and updates.

Your choices:

  • In the EU, EEA and UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Uninstalling SoundShare stops all data collection by the app. To ask about data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls.

5 Data Retention & Security

Shared audio exists only in memory for the moment it is streamed and is never retained. Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

6 Children's Privacy

SoundShare is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.
